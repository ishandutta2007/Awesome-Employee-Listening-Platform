Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on Employee Engagement, Employee Surveys, Pulse Surveys, eNPS, Workplace Culture, People Analytics & Continuous Listening
Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable Employee Listening platforms and open-source projects for collecting, analyzing, and acting on employee feedback across engagement surveys, pulse surveys, eNPS, organizational culture, manager effectiveness, onboarding, wellbeing, inclusion, retention, and workplace experience.

Examples include Culture Amp, Peakon, Microsoft Viva Glint, Qualtrics EmployeeXM, WorkTango, Workleap Officevibe, Quantum Workplace, Perceptyx, WorkBuzz, TeamMood, Lattice, Leapsome, and TINYpulse.

Open-source emphasis: The Open-Source section prioritizes projects that can serve as direct or complementary alternatives to commercial employee-listening platforms. Since mature open-source equivalents of Culture Amp or Qualtrics EmployeeXM are relatively uncommon, the list includes self-hosted survey platforms, feedback systems, form builders, polling tools, analytics platforms, HR systems, and communication/workflow building blocks that can be assembled into a privacy-preserving employee-listening stack.

Contributions welcome! Please add missing employee-feedback platforms, open-source survey systems, people-analytics projects, feedback tools, and employee-engagement building blocks.

Open-Source GitHub Projects

LimeSurvey
One of the strongest open-source foundations for employee listening. LimeSurvey is a self-hostable survey platform supporting conditional logic, branching, multilingual surveys, anonymous surveys, statistics, APIs, templates, and advanced survey configuration. Its official materials specifically support employee engagement, satisfaction, culture, onboarding, leadership, eNPS, and other HR surveys.

Nextcloud Forms
Self-hosted forms and survey application for Nextcloud supporting survey creation, sharing, response visualization, CSV export, and webhooks. It keeps survey information and responses under the organization's control.

OpenSurvey
Open-source survey application suitable for creating questionnaires and collecting structured feedback. It can be adapted for employee pulse surveys and internal research.

OhMyForm
Open-source, self-hosted form builder inspired by commercial form platforms. Useful for lightweight employee questionnaires, anonymous feedback forms, internal polls, and pulse surveys.

Formbricks
Open-source feedback and survey platform supporting surveys, feedback collection, targeting, and analytics. While frequently used for product feedback, its infrastructure can also be adapted for internal employee listening.

HeyForm
Open-source form and survey platform designed for creating interactive forms and collecting responses. It can provide a self-hosted foundation for employee surveys and feedback workflows.

Fider
Open-source feedback-management platform originally designed for collecting and prioritizing product feedback. Its voting, discussion, categorization, and feedback-management model can be adapted for internal employee suggestions and organizational feedback.

Feedbackly
Open feedback-oriented tooling and survey concepts can be combined with self-hosted survey infrastructure to build internal employee-feedback workflows.

Limesurvey Community Edition
Particularly suitable for privacy-sensitive employee listening because the Community Edition can be deployed on organizational infrastructure rather than requiring survey responses to reside entirely in a third-party SaaS environment. LimeSurvey explicitly documents employee engagement and HR survey use cases.

Framework for building a self-hosted Employee Listening platform: Combine LimeSurvey / Nextcloud Forms / Formbricks / OhMyForm / HeyForm for survey and pulse collection; Fider for continuous suggestions and persistent feedback; PostgreSQL / Supabase for employee-listening data; n8n / Node-RED / Airflow for HRIS synchronization and workflow automation; Metabase / Apache Superset / Grafana for engagement dashboards; pandas / scikit-learn / BERTopic / Sentence Transformers for quantitative and qualitative analysis; and Ollama / Hugging Face Transformers / Open WebUI for privacy-preserving AI analysis of open-ended employee comments.

For organizational communication and action management, add Nextcloud / Mattermost / Rocket.Chat / Discourse to distribute surveys, communicate results, run employee communities, and close the feedback loop.

This modular architecture can cover survey creation → anonymous/pseudonymous collection → pulse surveys → eNPS → demographic/team segmentation → qualitative comment analysis → engagement-driver analysis → manager dashboards → action planning → recurring listening → longitudinal organizational analytics, while allowing sensitive employee-feedback data to remain under organizational control.

Disclaimer

This is a curated ecosystem rather than a ranking or endorsement.

Commercial employee-listening platforms differ substantially in survey methodology, benchmarking, anonymity controls, analytics, organizational hierarchies, integrations, AI capabilities, manager workflows, and employee-experience functionality.

Some projects in the Open-Source section are direct survey/feedback platforms, while others are analytics tools, NLP frameworks, collaboration systems, HR platforms, workflow engines, or infrastructure components that can form part of an employee-listening solution.

Open-source survey software does not necessarily provide the validated engagement models, normative benchmarks, organizational psychology research, or people-science capabilities available from specialized commercial platforms.

Employee-feedback systems can contain highly sensitive organizational and personal information. Privacy, anonymity, access controls, data retention, encryption, and organizational security requirements should be carefully evaluated before deployment.

Open-source availability, licensing, features, supported integrations, and project activity can change over time.
